A race weekend, post by post

A competition runs for four or five days, and the audience follows in fragments. This run sheet plans what to publish and when, so the story builds instead of piling up on Sunday night.

ThursdayScrutineering

  • Time-lapse of the car going through technical inspection
  • Story poll: will it pass first time?
Goal: build anticipation. Track story replies and saves.

FridayStatic events

  • Carousel: the toughest question the design judges asked
  • Team member spotlight, one per post
Goal: show the brains behind the car. Track shares.

SaturdayDynamic events

  • Live stories from the pit and the track edge
  • Short clips of each run, with the time on screen
Goal: real-time energy. Track reach and story completion.

SundayEndurance and awards

  • Lap-by-lap updates during the 22 km run
  • Result graphic and a thank-you to sponsors
Goal: the payoff moment. Track total engagement.

MondayRecap

  • Highlight reel under 60 seconds
  • Three lessons for next season
Goal: keep the audience for the next event. Track follows.

Every lap has a story

Behind each result are months of late nights, failed tests and a lot of coffee. Telling that story well is part of the race.
